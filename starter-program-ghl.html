Membership details

Everything included in Starter

Starter is built to make the beginning feel clear and manageable. You are not expected to have everything figured out before you start. The program gives you direction, support, and a place to keep improving.

Defensive Shooting University Online Access

Study the program material, understand the training path, and prepare for live instruction with more confidence.

Telegram Group Chat Access

Stay connected with the JM Training community, ask questions, and keep training on your mind between events.

At-Your-Own-Pace Roadmap

Follow a structured advancement roadmap without being rushed into standards you are not ready for yet.

Group Training Events

Attend group training events in Frankfort that reinforce safety, fundamentals, and responsible progression.

Competition Nights

Participate in competition nights in Bourbonnais to test skills, manage pressure, and learn from feedback.

Monthly Gun Cleaning and Networking

Maintain equipment, ask practical questions, and stay connected through a recurring monthly member event.

Legal and Scenario Training

Access legal seminars and scenario training that connect defensive skill with judgment, restraint, and responsibility.

Try Guns and Gear Before Buying

Get hands-on exposure to different guns and gear before spending money on equipment that may not fit your needs.

Free USCCA Membership

Starter members receive a USCCA membership as part of a broader responsibility and preparedness framework.
